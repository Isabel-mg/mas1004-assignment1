MY CLEANING RULE (second round, written before running clean.py look/suspects on the new 1,168 images)
===============================================================================

An image stays only if it is an ordinary photograph of the OUTSOLE (the
bottom) of the right shoe, where the tread pattern is visible and its shape can
be told apart. I remove everything else.

WHAT EACH CATEGORY MUST SHOW
  converse_diamond_sole_shape          Converse sole with the diamond shape
  nike_air_force_circular_sole_shape   Nike Air Force sole with clean lines and
                                       circular shapes
  vans_honeycomb_sole_shape            Vans sole with the honeycomb shape

I REMOVE an image if it:
  1. shows the full shoe and the sole is out of frame, tiny or blurred
  2. shows something other than the outsole of a shoe (drawing, logo, text,
     collage, listing screenshot, placeholder, patent/CAD schematic)
  3. shows a sole that does not match the brand and the specific model sole
     of its category
  4. shows anything else that is not a shoe (tiles, flooring, cars, insoles,
     a booklet alone)
  5. shows a sole that is destroyed, covered or mixed with other objects, so
     that no pattern or shape can be seen
  6. is a near copy of an image I already keep

I KEEP (borderline cases I decided):
  - Angled soles, as long as part of the pattern is visible and its shape can
    be told apart.
  - Worn or dirty soles, unless no shape or pattern can be seen at all.
  - Two soles in one picture, if both are the correct brand and model.

REASON WORDS for `clean.py remove <class>/<file> ... --reason "<word>"`
(copied verbatim into data/removed/log.csv, so `clean.py count` reads straight
off this rule):
  no-tread       rule 1, and rule 5 when the sole is covered or too worn or
                 dirty to see any pattern
  not-a-photo    rule 2
  wrong-object   rule 3 (wrong brand, model or pattern) and rule 4 (not a shoe)
  duplicate      rule 6
  unreadable     a file the tools cannot open

BUDGET: check.py wants more than 50 images per class after cleaning, and the
biggest class must not exceed 3x the smallest. Target: at least 100 kept per
class. Report the final counts either way.
